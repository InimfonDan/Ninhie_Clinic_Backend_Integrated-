A little on how the clinic desk works, and what happens to your booking after you submit it.

How booking works here

Three simple steps, from account to confirmed visit.

Register once

Create a patient profile with your basic details. You'll use your phone number or email to log back in next time.

Request a slot

Pick a department, a doctor, and an open time slot. The system blocks double-bookings on the same doctor automatically.

Wait for approval

Every request starts as Pending. Our front desk reviews it and marks it Approved or Cancelled — you'll see the update the next time you check your dashboard.

Departments

Doctors are organized into departments by the clinic desk, and new departments can be created at any time.
